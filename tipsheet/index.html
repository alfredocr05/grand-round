Getting the most out of Evidently

Evidently is the second tab in Chart Review. It reads the whole chart, including outside records and the media tab, and writes summaries that link back to the notes they came from. The rest takes a few minutes, once.

Where it lives

Open any chart. In Chart Review, the Evidently tab sits beside the others. The Overview shows summary cards that refresh themselves; the timestamp on each card tells you when. Each card links to the notes it read and has a copy icon.

Seven things worth knowing

  1. Manage Layout. Choose which Collection appears on your Overview, drag the cards into the order you want, then Save.
  2. Add summaries. Inside Manage Layout, open the library panel to tick or untick summaries. Changes save on their own; Done closes the panel.
  3. Custom summary. Click + Custom summary, write a rough prompt in plain words, and click Polish to turn it into a clean one. Preview, adjust, then Save and close. It lands in your current Collection.
  4. Edit anything. The pencil on a card opens its detail view: title, description, prompt, which Collections it belongs to, and whether it is shared with UIHC.
  5. Your library. Click the Summaries header on the Overview, or find Summaries in the start menu. Manage My Summaries lists yours; search and filter to find one quickly.
  6. The UIHC library. Browse summaries colleagues have shared. Preview the output, add it to a Collection, or Make a Copy to make it yours.
  7. Ask Evidently. Ask one question about this chart. The answer comes with citations; click one to read the source note.

Add the anesthesia huddle

In the UIHC library, search for the anesthesia huddle and add it to your Overview. From then on it is on every chart you open: the next procedure, a short narrative, the systems in a fixed order, the core lab table with Not available where a result is missing, and the count of records and data points it read.
